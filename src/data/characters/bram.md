## Look
Tall, a white head with dark spiky hair, big-pupil eyes, a mustard bean shirt, dark shoes. Taller than Tess; the louder silhouette of the pair.

## Voice
Dev voice: macOS `say` "Fred"; Kokoro `am_onyx` (low). For posting, the tamtree harness picks the TTS voice, or a person records. Delivery: low, slow, sure of himself, deadpan when he's losing.

## Personality
Lives by rules he made up and will defend to the end ("the armrest goes to whoever sat first"). Confident, never shouts first, doubles down when cornered. Sometimes he's right, and that's what makes the comments fight.

## Wants
To win on a technicality, and to never pay for something he didn't order.

## Physical comedy
Arms crossed, hands on hips, a slow point. Shrugs off a shove. Recoil only on the twist.

- Obviously wrong. Every episode he has a real point, or there is no debate.
