# Tess

> **Voice** come from `tess.json`. **Personality**, **Wants**, **Catchphrases** and **Never** are
> a starting draft for the "Who's Wrong?" channel (status below); the team owns them.

Status: draft, team to confirm

## Look
A white head with an auburn bun, big-pupil eyes, a teal bean shirt, dark shoes. A little shorter than Bram.

## Voice
Dev voice: macOS `say` "Karen"; Kokoro `af_aoede`. For posting, the tamtree harness picks the TTS voice, or a person records. Delivery: quick, precise, rising when she has the receipt.

## Personality
Keeps receipts, literally. Calm until she has proof, then fast. Fair in principle, petty in practice ("I'm not mad, I'm just keeping track"). She is wrong about as often as Bram.

## Wants
For the rule to be applied to everyone, especially Bram.

## Physical comedy
Holds up the phone or the receipt, points, a sharp head turn to camera. Facepalm when Bram finds a loophole.

- The automatic winner. The audience must be able to side with Bram.
