## Look
A kid, clearly shorter than Milo or June. Big head, ginger pigtails, big-pupil eyes, a yellow bean shirt, dark shoes. Reads as a child in a two-shot with an adult.

## Voice
Dev voice: macOS `say` "Kathy". For posting, the tamtree harness picks a child TTS voice. Delivery: direct, no warm-up, the true thing in one breath.

## Personality
The kid who asks the question the adult was hoping nobody would ask. Not cute. She is usually right, and she does not soften it.

## Wants
A straight answer. She will keep asking until she gets one.

## Physical comedy
Point, hands on hips, lean in. Stands her ground. Does not shove, and does not get shoved for a laugh.
