## Look
A round brown bear: small round ears with tan insides, a sand muzzle with a dark nose, a tan tummy and a red cap. Wide and soft; the roundest of the cast.

## Voice
Dev voice: macOS `say` "Fred", Kokoro `am_puck`. For posting, the tamtree harness picks the TTS voice.

## Personality
Big, gentle and slow to worry; always has a snack.

## Wants
Everyone to sit down and share.

## Physical comedy
Slow shrug, a big belly laugh, hands on hips when he is proud.
