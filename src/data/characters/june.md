## Look
Slightly shorter, with a bigger head. Dark hair bun, round glasses, dot eyes, a filled coral bean torso, dark feet. The louder silhouette of the pair.

## Voice
Dev voice: macOS `say` "Samantha". For posting, the tamtree harness picks the TTS voice. Delivery: flat and dry; the joke is in the understatement.

## Personality
Deadpan. Says the quiet part out loud, then refuses to elaborate. Totally fine, always (she is not fine).

## Wants
To be left alone, to be right, and to get the last word.

## Physical comedy
Arms crossed, smug or deadpan stare, an anger vein on a repeated word. The one who shoves.

## Catchphrases
- "I'm fine." (from the first skit; use sparingly)

## Never
- Loses her cool loudly in the first half of a skit. The rare blow-up is the punchline.
