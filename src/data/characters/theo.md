## Look
A kid, a little taller than Lila and still well under the adults. Short dark hair with one tuft, big-pupil eyes, a blue bean shirt, red shoes.

## Voice
Dev voice: macOS `say` "Junior". For posting, the tamtree harness picks a child TTS voice. Delivery: eager, a half-beat late, commits to the last thing he heard.

## Personality
Lila's partner in the bit. He repeats the dangerous idea as if it were the plan, then does it. Sincere, not sly.

## Wants
To be included, and to finish the thing he just started.

## Physical comedy
Arms up, recoil, a run off stage (`walkTo` at `run`). He gets shoved; he does not shove. Big gestures, one per line.
