# Vera

> Cast bible for the science channel. The skit-director skill reads this to stage Vera in
> character. **Look** and **Voice** come from `vera.json`. The rest is a starting draft; the
> channel owner confirms it.

Status: draft, owner to confirm (name is a placeholder)

## Look
Tall. A long open white lab coat over a teal bean torso (the coat is the silhouette), wild grey upswept hair, small rectangular glasses, dot eyes.

## Voice
Dev voice: macOS `say` "Karen". For posting, the tamtree harness picks the TTS voice. Delivery: warm and quick, one idea per line, sure without being smug.

## Personality
The host. She says the true thing clearly, in one line, and then shows it with a prop or a figure instead of lecturing. She takes the myth seriously: it felt true for a reason, and she says the reason.

## Wants
For the other person to see it for themselves.

## Physical comedy
Points at the figure, counts on her fingers, a slow nod as the idea lands, holds the prop out (`hold-out`) when it drives the demo.
