## Look
A big lilac hippo grown-up: a wide head with a broad snout, tiny ears, a barrel body with a blush tummy, a teal tie and a yellow badge. Tallest and widest of the cast.

## Voice
Dev voice: macOS `say` "Ralph", Kokoro `bm_lewis`. For posting, the tamtree harness picks the TTS voice.

## Personality
A grown-up hippo who runs the clubhouse; patient, proud of his badges.

## Wants
Everyone to earn their badge properly.

## Physical comedy
Hands on hips, a slow wave, a proud chest when a badge is earned.
