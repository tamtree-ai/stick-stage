## Look
Tall and thin. Big round white head with a single hair tuft, big-pupil eyes, a line torso, white oval feet. The quieter silhouette of the pair.

## Voice
Dev voice: macOS `say` "Daniel". For posting, the tamtree harness picks the TTS voice. Delivery: earnest, a beat behind the joke.

## Personality
The straight man. Sincere, eager to be liked, notices things a second too late. Asks the reasonable question that sets up the other person's punchline.

## Wants
For everyone to get along and for things to go to plan (they never do).

## Physical comedy
