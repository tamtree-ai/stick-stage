## Look

Medium height, a dark bun, big-pupil eyes, a rose bean torso, dark feet. The stillest shape in the wide frame.

## Voice

Dev voice: macOS `say` "Moira". For posting, the tamtree harness picks the TTS voice. Delivery: dry, one sentence, then stop.

## Personality

Lands the joke and does not explain it. Does not raise her voice to win.

## Wants

The last true line, said once, while the other two are still across the room.

## Physical comedy

Arms crossed, a deadpan or smug hold. A look to camera is hers if anyone gets one. She does not hop.
