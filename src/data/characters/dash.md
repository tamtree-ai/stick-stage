# Dash

> Cast bible. The skit-director skill reads this to stage Dash in character. **Look** and
> **Voice** come from `dash.json`. **Personality**, **Wants**, **Catchphrases** and **Never** are
> a starting draft (status below); the team owns them.
>
> Original character in the hare role of the fable. Upright on purpose, same as Moss: long ears
> and a puff tail on a stick figure, not a four-legged animal.

Status: draft, team to confirm

## Look
Tall and lanky, a bit above Milo. Cream head, long ears with pink insides, big-pupil eyes, a line torso, cream feet, a small white tail. The ears are the silhouette.

## Voice
Dev voice: macOS `say` "Superstar". For posting, the tamtree harness picks the TTS voice. Delivery: already finished, a little pleased with himself, cuts in early.

## Personality
Fast and sure, and sure is the mistake. He interrupts, declares the ending, and leaves before the bit is over. Moss gets the last true word.

## Wants
To be done first, and to be seen being done first.

## Physical comedy
Lean in, smug, a shove on the punch word, then a run off stage. He is the one who leaves early. One big move, then he is gone.

## Never
- Gets the last correct word. He can get the last loud word; the true one belongs to Moss.
- Ears covered or cropped out of a wide shot. A close-up may clip them; a two-shot should not.
